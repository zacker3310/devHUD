# Spec: side pill HUD

**id:** T2   **intent:** .buildloop/intent/T2.md
**policies applied:** T1 spec tokens; root CLAUDE.md style
**status:** accepted

## Requirements
R1. `HUDPanel`: borderless non-activating `NSPanel`, clear background, level `.statusBar`,
    joins all Spaces and full-screen auxiliary, never key or main, frame never constrained to the screen.
R2. Pill panel docked with its right edge at `NSScreen.screens.first.frame.maxX`, vertical
    center at 58 percent of the screen height from the bottom. Repositions on screen change.
R3. States: concealed (pill translated right, `peek` points visible), revealed (fully on screen),
    card open (revealed plus a second panel). Auto-hide is a UserDefaults bool, default on;
    off means always revealed.
R4. The cursor is tracked with mouse-move monitors and resolved against the two window
    frames (`HoverResolver`). Cursor on the pill reveals it immediately; cursor off both the
    pill and the card conceals after 0.8 s, closing any open card first. Concealed, the
    window is `peek` points wide and never extends past the screen edge. The pill does not
    conceal while its menu is open. (Demo video, 2026-09-04.)
R5. Cursor on a ring opens that provider's card and moving to another ring swaps it; the
    badge opens the Dev Servers card the same way. Leaving the ring closes the card after
    0.35 s unless the cursor is on the card or another ring. Rings take no clicks; the gear
    is the only click target. The card's pointer is on its trailing edge at the hovered
    item's vertical center.
R6. Pill content top to bottom: Claude, Copilot, Codex rings with percent labels, server badge
    with count, gear. Gear opens an `NSMenu`: Keep Pill Visible (checked when the pill does not hide), Refresh Now, Quit.
R7. DynamicNotchKit removed from project.yml; no other dependency.
R8. Slide uses `NSAnimationContext` on the window frame (0.28 s ease-out) so hit-testing
    matches what is drawn. Both panels use the window's own shadow, so transparent pixels
    pass clicks through to the app underneath.

## Acceptance criteria
AC1. build + tests green (existing 32 plus any new) | CHECK:build, CHECK:tests
AC2. screenshot: pill at the right edge, three rings with percents, badge, gear | CHECK:live-pill
AC3. screenshot: card open to the left with pointer, after a simulated click on a ring | CHECK:live-card
AC4. `swift package`/project has no DynamicNotchKit reference | CHECK:no-dnk

## Ordering surface
state-machine: concealed / revealed / card, driven by hover, click, timer, and
outside-click monitors, all on the main actor via one `HUDController`.

## Rejected alternatives
- One large transparent panel with SwiftUI offsets: blocks clicks on the transparent area.
- Keeping DynamicNotchKit for notch Macs: the side pill is the product now.
