# Spec: pill placement

**id:** T6   **intent:** .buildloop/intent/T6.md
**status:** accepted

## Requirements
R1. `PillPlacement { edge: left|right, anchor: 0...1 (center y fraction from the screen bottom), screenName }`
    persisted in UserDefaults (`pillEdge`, `pillAnchor`, `pillScreen`). Default right, 0.58, nil (main).
R2. `PillMetric.pillFrame(placement:screen:revealed:rows:)` is pure: concealed width is `peek` at the
    docked edge; the anchor is clamped so the pill stays within the screen.
R3. `PillPlacement.snapped(cursor:screen:screenName:)`: edge is whichever the cursor is nearer; anchor
    from the cursor's y fraction. The screen is the one containing the cursor.
R4. Drag: mouse down on the pill (not on the gear) starts tracking; after 4 pt of movement the pill
    follows the cursor unclipped, timers cancel and the card closes; on release the placement snaps,
    saves, animates into place and hover re-resolves. Less than 4 pt is a click, untouched.
R5. Left edge mirrors `SidePillShape`; the hosting view keeps its trailing edge at the window's trailing
    edge when concealed on the left. The card pops out to the right with the pointer on its leading edge.
R6. Gear menu: Dock Left, Dock Right (radio), Display submenu (radio over `NSScreen.localizedName`),
    Reset Position. MenuBarExtra mirrors Dock and Display.
R7. Screen-parameter changes re-place the pill on the chosen screen, or main if it is gone.

## Acceptance criteria
AC1. build + tests green (frames for both edges, snap edge choice, anchor clamp, card x) | CHECK:tests
AC2. live: `dock:left` shows the mirrored pill at the left edge with a card to its right | CHECK:live-left
AC3. live: `anchor:0.2` moves it down the edge | CHECK:live-anchor
AC4. relaunch keeps the placement | CHECK:live-persist
