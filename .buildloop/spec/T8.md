# Spec: Codenotch parity

**id:** T8   **intent:** .buildloop/intent/T8.md
**status:** accepted

## Requirements
R1. `UsageBand` from percent used: ample under 50, watch under 70, critical under 100, exhausted at
    100. Ring and bar colours come from the band (the existing green, yellow, orange triad); the
    glyph identifies the provider. Exhausted dims the glyph.
R2. `UsageFormat.resetText`: under 60 min "Resets in N min"; within 7 days "Resets Thu 12:00 AM";
    beyond "Resets Sep 10". Injected now and calendar; tested.
R3. Live sessions. `ClaudeSessionMonitor` reads `~/.claude/sessions/*.json` (pid, cwd, status,
    startedAt, name, entrypoint), keeps only records whose pid is alive with a matching start time,
    watches the directory and rescans every 5 s. `CodexActivityMonitor` treats a rollout file written
    in the last 8 s as a busy session. `ActivityStore` publishes sessions and a summary state:
    working, waiting, idle.
R4. Ring overlay: a thin arc spins inside a ring while its provider is working; a pulsing amber ring
    while waiting on the user. The provider card lists live sessions: name, surface, folder, state,
    since.
R5. Usage polling: 120 s while any agent session is live, 300 s otherwise. Retry-After still wins.
R6. Stale: a snapshot over an hour old, or a rate-limited provider, dims its ring to 55 percent.
R7. Click a ring to pin its card; the pinned card ignores close and conceal timers until the ring is
    clicked again, another ring is clicked, or the pill conceals by Hide.
R8. "Hide for 1 Hour" in the gear menu and on right-click of the pill: conceal and ignore hover
    until the hour passes or the menu bar toggle is used.
R9. Cursor provider when `~/Library/Application Support/Cursor/User/globalStorage/state.vscdb`
    yields `cursorAuth/accessToken` and `cursorAuth/stripeMembershipAuthId`: GET
    `https://cursor.com/api/usage-summary` with the WorkosCursorSessionToken cookie; windows from
    `individualUsage.plan.totalPercentUsed`, `apiPercentUsed` when over 0, `onDemand` used/limit;
    reset from `billingCycleEnd`. Read-only SQLite open. No slot when the DB or token is absent.
