# Intent: side-notch pill that floats and hides

**id:** T2   **originator:** Zac (screenshot of Codenotch, session 2026-09-04)   **date:** 2026-09-04
**status:** accepted

## Problem
T1 put the HUD in the top notch surface (DynamicNotchKit) and, on this
notch-less Mac, a floating panel under the menu bar. The reference Zac wants is
Codenotch's side notch: a vertical black pill docked to the right screen edge
with the three rings stacked, percentages under each, a gear at the bottom, that
tucks away when idle and pops a detail card out to the left when a ring is tapped.

## Desired outcome
The pill sits on the right edge, upper-middle of the screen, above other
windows and on every Space. Idle, it slides right so only a sliver shows. Hover
slides it in. Tapping a ring opens that provider's card with a pointer to the
ring; tapping again or clicking anywhere else closes it. The dev-server badge
opens the Dev Servers card the same way. The gear opens a menu.

## Constraints
- Same stores and providers as T1. No new data work.
- Black pill, same tokens, notch-style concave ears where it meets the edge.
- Read-only. Menu: Auto-hide toggle, Refresh, Quit.

## Open questions
Peek width and vertical position are eyeballed from the screenshots.

## Not doing
Drag to reposition, per-screen placement, left-edge docking, animations beyond
slide and fade.
