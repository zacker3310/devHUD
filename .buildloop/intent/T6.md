# Intent: move the pill

**id:** T6   **originator:** Zac ("implement both of the moving tasks: right, left, up down, a different window")   **date:** 2026-09-04
**status:** accepted

## Problem
The pill is pinned to one spot on one edge of one display.

## Desired outcome
Drag the pill up or down its edge, drag it across to the other edge or onto
another display and it snaps there; or pick Dock Left / Dock Right and the
display from the gear menu. The card pops out from whichever edge the pill is
on. Position survives relaunch and display changes.

## Not doing
Top or bottom edges. Free-floating placement away from an edge.
