## Risks
- Claude endpoint shape unverified (spike blocked). Mitigation: generic decoder,
  raw body logged at debug on decode failure. Wrong shape shows `--`, not a crash.
- Xcode 27 beta may reject macOS 14 deployment with new SwiftUI APIs. Mitigation:
  use only APIs available since 14 (`@Observable` is 14+).
- `gh` not on the app's PATH. Mitigation: search fixed candidate paths.
- Floating style ignores compact views; on this machine only expanded is testable.

## Departures
- `Shell` and `ISO8601` helpers live in `App/Providers/UsageProvider.swift`
  rather than their own files; `HUDLog` lives in `AIUsageStore.swift`.
- CHECK:run-log dropped. `log show` returned nothing for the process even with
  `--info`; the live check is the screenshot (CHECK:live) instead.
- `ProcessInspector.resources` first shipped with a pointer-to-pointer
  `proc_pid_rusage` call that overflowed the stack and aborted both the app and
  the test host. Fixed; recorded in CLAUDE.md common mistakes.
- Added `stale` to `WindowRow` after the live check: a Codex snapshot from
  Aug 18 rendered "Resets now", which is noise on stale data.
- Added `ardagent` to the lsof denylist after it appeared in the live list.
- `AppDelegate` is `@MainActor`.
